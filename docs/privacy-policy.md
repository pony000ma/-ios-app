# 多巴胺外卖隐私政策

生效日期：2026-07-03

多巴胺外卖是一款本机运行的外卖流程模拟 App，用于帮助用户在冲动消费前获得情绪缓冲。它不提供真实外卖、真实支付、真实配送、真实商家或地图服务。

## 我们收集的数据

我们不会收集、上传、出售、共享或追踪用户数据。

App 内的虚拟用户名、虚拟余额、购物车、虚拟订单状态、配色偏好和复盘历史只保存在用户设备本地的 `UserDefaults` 中，不会传输到服务器。

## 权限与第三方服务

本 App 不请求定位、相机、照片、通讯录、麦克风、蓝牙、健康、日历、提醒事项或 App Tracking Transparency 追踪权限。

本 App 不集成广告 SDK、分析 SDK、崩溃统计 SDK、支付 SDK 或第三方账号登录 SDK。

## 网络与支付

本 App 不向后端 API 发送请求，不上传用户内容，不处理真实付款，也不销售数字内容。App 内出现的地址、余额、订单、骑手和配送状态都是虚拟模拟内容。

## 儿童隐私

由于本 App 不收集用户数据，我们也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

## 政策变更

如果未来版本的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本隐私政策，并在 App Store Connect 中同步更新隐私申报。

## 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 App Store 页面提供的开发者联系方式联系我们。

# Dopamine Delivery Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-03

Dopamine Delivery is an on-device food-delivery simulation app designed to help users pause before impulse spending. It does not provide real food delivery, real payment, real merchants, real riders, or map services.

## Data We Collect

We do not collect, upload, sell, share, or track user data.

Virtual usernames, virtual wallet balances, cart state, simulated order state, color preferences, and reflection history are stored only on the user's device using `UserDefaults`. This data is not transmitted to any server.

## Permissions and Third-Party Services

The app does not request location, camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, health, calendar, reminders, or App Tracking Transparency permissions.

The app does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, payment SDKs, or third-party sign-in SDKs.

## Network and Payment

The app does not send requests to a backend API, upload user content, process real payments, or sell digital content. Addresses, wallet balances, orders, riders, and delivery states shown in the app are simulated.

## Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect user data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version changes the app's data practices, we will update this policy and the App Store Connect privacy responses accordingly.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us using the developer contact information provided on the App Store product page.
